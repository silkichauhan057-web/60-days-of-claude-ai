      Your AI stack

      Three ways to build the stack depending on budget and team size.

      Solo · Free-to-cheap

      Bootstrap kit

      • Claude or ChatGPT free tier — strategy, captions, scripts
      • Canva free — carousels, story graphics
      • CapCut free — Reel editing & auto-captions
      • Instagram native Insights — analytics
      • Meta Business Suite — scheduling, one inbox
      ~$0–20/mo
      Growth · Small team

      Full pipeline

      • Claude Pro — strategy docs, reports, brand-voice memory
      • Midjourney or Adobe Firefly — product & lifestyle imagery
      • Canva Pro (Magic Studio) — on-brand templated design
      • CapCut Pro / Runway — polished Reels
      • Flick — hashtag & caption AI
      • Later or Metricool — scheduling + analytics AI
      • Manychat — DM automation
      ~$150–350/mo
      Scale · Paid + automated

      Performance stack

      • Everything in Growth, plus:
      • Meta Advantage+ — AI ad targeting & creative
      • AdCreative.ai — ad variant generation at volume
      • Repurpose.io — cross-platform auto-posting
      • Zapier or Make — content-ops automation glue
      • Sprout Social — unified inbox, team reporting
      ~$500–1500+/mo
